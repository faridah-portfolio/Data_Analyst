# FARIDA ABUBAKAR DATA ANALYTICS PORTFOLIO
## PROJECT 1 :SALES PERFORMANCE OVERVIEW DASHBOARD 
This project involves the development of an interactive Excel dashboard designed to analyze sales performance data for the year 2025. The dashboard provides a clear and concise overview of key business metrics, including total sales, total profit, and units sold, while enabling dynamic exploration through interactive filters.
### Business Questions Addressed
What is the overall business performance in terms of sales, profit, and units sold?
How do sales trends evolve over time?
Which product categories contribute the most to total sales?
How does performance vary across different product categories?
How do filters such as product, region, and salesperson influence overall performance?
