# PROJECT 1: Sales Performance Overview Dashboard (2025)
This project involves the development of an interactive Excel dashboard designed to analyze sales performance data for the year 2025. The dashboard provides a clear and concise overview of key business metrics, including total sales, total profit, and units sold, while enabling dynamic exploration through interactive filters.
## BUSINESS QUESTION ADDRESSED 
 Which region generated the highest sales and profit during the year?
 
• Who are the top three performing salespersons based on profit?

• Which product contributed the most to overall sales across all regions?

• What is the monthly trend of sales and profit throughout 2025?

• How does profit vary by product and region when filtered by salesperson?
